# mistakes.md

One line per correction. Format: `YYYY-MM-DD | what I did | what I should have done`.
Graduate to `.claude/skills/<name>/SKILL.md` on the second occurrence or when the
fix needs more than one line, then delete the entry. Entries older than 30 days that
have not graduated are surfaced by lint for the human to decide.
